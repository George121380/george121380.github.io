Research

I study how robots can learn new skills from limited experience and adapt to unfamiliar tasks. My work spans world action models, dexterous manipulation, and lifelong learning.

I want to build robots that learn efficiently and help with everyday physical work, connecting learning algorithms with robot hardware.
