Research

How can robots learn new skills from limited experience and apply them to unfamiliar tasks? My work spans world action models, dexterous manipulation, and lifelong learning, with a focus on efficient skill acquisition and adaptation.

My long-term goal is to build robots that learn as efficiently as humans and help with everyday physical work. I’m pursuing this across learning algorithms and robot hardware.
